## Story structure (Taglish — t03/t02)

1. **Act 1 — Loneliness**: Marco sits alone. *"Two years na ako dito... hindi pa
   rin feels like home."*
2. **Act 2 — The call**: Nanay tells him to get Chickenjoy. *"Kumain ka na ba?
   Go get some Chickenjoy, anak. Parang nandito lang kami."*
3. **Act 3 — The journey**: Marco walks through rain to the store. *"Welcome po!
   One Chickenjoy meal, sir?"*
4. **Act 4 — The bite**: One bite triggers family memory. *"Isang kagat... and
   I'm back at our table. Yung ngiti ni Papa. Yung tawa ni Nanay."*
5. **Act 5 — Resolution**: Marco is reassured. *"Salamat, Ma. Okay na ako."*
   Announcer: *"Chickenjoy. Crispy. Juicy. Langhap-sarap. Because no matter how
   far you go — home is just one bite away."*
