# Scene 01 — Home Is Just One Bite Away

## Story

Marco, a young Filipino working abroad in Singapore, sits alone on a rainy
night. Homesick and tired, he calls his mother (Nanay). She tells him to go
get some Chickenjoy — it'll feel like she's right there with him. He walks
through the rain to a nearby store, orders a Chickenjoy meal, and takes a
bite. The crunch of the crispy skin triggers a joyful memory of family
dinners — his father's smile, his mother's laugh. The memory fades back to
the store. Marco smiles, reassured. The announcer delivers the tagline:
"Chickenjoy. Crispy. Juicy. Langhap-sarap. Because no matter how far you go —
home is just one bite away."

## Cast

- **Marco** — late 20s male, Filipino English accent, warm but tired, slightly

- **Nanay** — late 50s female, warm gentle Filipino accent, nurturing, slightly
  raspy with age. Marco's mother (phone call). Speaks Tagalog-heavy Taglish.
- **Crew member** — young female, cheerful, bright energetic. Store crew.
- **Announcer** — deep, warm, confident male. Brand voiceover.

- **jollibee-store** — warm, busy, welcoming.
- **family-memory** — joyful dinner table (flashback).
