# Animation Style Recipes

Use one recipe at a time. Adapt its craft logic to the user's subject and
story; do not paste the full bank into a prompt. The medium lock must govern
characters, props, sets, environmental effects, and transformations.

## Cubist ink paint

- **ID:** `cubist-ink-paint`
- **Aliases:** cubist illustration, painted ink animation, angular storybook
- **Medium lock:** Hand-painted ink and opaque brushwork on textured paper with angular, selectively fractured geometry.
- **Construction:** Characters and sets use elongated painted shapes, ink contours, layered washes, and restrained cubist perspective shifts.
- **Surface behavior:** Brush drag, paper tooth, uneven pigment density, and dry-brush edges remain visible.
- **Motion cadence:** Fluid theatrical motion punctuated by controlled perspective bends and painterly redraw.
- **Deformation rules:** Forms may facet or shift viewpoint for emphasis but keep identity, limb count, and prop ownership stable.
- **Environment inheritance:** Light, smoke, dust, and transitions appear as painted washes, loose ink marks, or moving paper texture.
- **Imperfection cues:** Visible brushstrokes; ink-edge wobble; paper grain; slight registration drift.
- **Compatible tones:** Reflective drama, magical realism, theatrical performance, literary fantasy.
- **Prompt opening:** `Hand-painted ink animation with restrained cubist geometry, elongated illustrated forms, visible brush drag, muted layered washes, textured paper grain, and fluid theatrical motion.`
- **Style seal:** `Hand-painted ink animation with subtle cubist perspective shifts, visible brushwork and paper texture, fluid theatrical motion, and controlled painterly redraw.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Clean vector surfaces, glossy photorealism, hard 3D geometry, logos unless requested.
- **Common failure modes:** Generic watercolor; faces changing during perspective shifts; abstract transformation overpowering the story.

## Wood puppet stop motion

- **ID:** `wood-puppet-stop-motion`
- **Aliases:** carved puppet, marionette stop motion, wooden miniature animation
- **Medium lock:** Carved wooden puppets with cloth costumes performing in practical miniature sets.
- **Construction:** Bodies use carved segments, visible hinges or pinned joints, painted faces, fiber hair, sewn garments, and handmade props.
- **Surface behavior:** Tool marks, worn paint, wood grain, fabric weave, and joint gaps stay visible.
- **Motion cadence:** Deliberate stepped stop-motion timing with small settling movements after each gesture.
- **Deformation rules:** Rigid wooden parts rotate at joints; cloth folds and hair lag behind; faces use subtle replacement pieces rather than clay-like stretching.
- **Environment inheritance:** Doors, stairs, flowers, weather, and practical effects use wood, cloth, paper, wire, or glass appropriate to a miniature stage.
- **Imperfection cues:** Carving marks; exposed joints; fabric seams; slight frame jitter.
- **Compatible tones:** Folklore, awkward romance, eerie fable, quiet comedy.
- **Prompt opening:** `Handcrafted carved-wood puppetry in a practical miniature set, with visible wood grain and joint pins, sewn cloth costumes, tool marks, softly worn painted surfaces, and deliberate stepped movement.`
- **Style seal:** `Carved-wood puppet stop motion with rigid joint mechanics, cloth follow-through, practical miniature materials, tool marks, and deliberate stepped timing.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Plasticine fingerprints, rubbery facial stretching, invisible joints, glossy modern CGI surfaces.
- **Common failure modes:** Drifting into claymation; joints disappearing; human skin replacing painted wood; overly fluid motion.

## Toy miniature

- **ID:** `toy-miniature`
- **Aliases:** action-figure animation, block-toy film, practical toy world
- **Medium lock:** Articulated plastic or block toys inside a practical miniature environment.
- **Construction:** Characters keep molded seams, fixed accessories, visible articulation, simplified hands, and toy-scale props.
- **Surface behavior:** Plastic reflections, paint rub, molded texture, tiny dust, and shallow miniature depth of field.
- **Motion cadence:** Purposeful toy-like posing with rigid transitions, clean holds, and occasional stop-motion irregularity.
- **Deformation rules:** Parts rotate at manufactured joints; hard plastic does not bend like flesh; soft plush compresses according to stuffing.
- **Environment inheritance:** Vehicles, architecture, debris, smoke, and light sources read at toy scale; real-world objects may reveal scale without changing the toys.
- **Imperfection cues:** Mold seams; exposed joints; small paint wear; practical-set dust.
- **Compatible tones:** Adventure, neo-noir parody, playful epic, childhood fantasy.
- **Prompt opening:** `Cinematic practical toy-miniature animation with articulated plastic figures, visible molded joints and paint wear, realistic toy-scale reflections, rigid posing, and shallow miniature depth of field.`
- **Style seal:** `Cinematic toy-miniature animation with visible articulation, rigid plastic mechanics, practical-set imperfections, expressive toy posing, and consistent scale.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Human skin, flexible fingers, disappearing joints, full-scale architecture unless used as a scale reveal.
- **Common failure modes:** Toys becoming people; inconsistent scale after a reverse shot; accessories changing between cuts.

## Needle-felt stop motion

- **ID:** `needle-felt-stop-motion`
- **Aliases:** felt animation, wool stop motion, stitched-fabric miniature
- **Medium lock:** Needle-felted wool and stitched fabrics with embroidered details and practical miniature construction.
- **Construction:** Bodies, props, landscapes, clouds, and waves use layered felt, wool roving, thread, burlap, embroidery, and small practical armatures.
- **Surface behavior:** Loose fibers, needle marks, seams, fuzzy edges, compressed wool, and thread tension remain visible.
- **Motion cadence:** Satisfying stepped stop-motion movement with slight fiber flutter and soft settling.
- **Deformation rules:** Wool compresses, bends, and springs softly; stitched parts flex at seams; forms do not melt like clay.
- **Environment inheritance:** Foam, spray, clouds, smoke, lightning, and debris become wool tufts, stitched shapes, thread, beads, or translucent practical pieces.
- **Imperfection cues:** Visible fibers; stitched seams; needle marks; subtle frame jitter.
- **Compatible tones:** Whimsical fantasy, tactile adventure, cozy drama, handcrafted spectacle.
- **Prompt opening:** `Needle-felted stop-motion animation made from layered wool and stitched fabrics, with visible fibers and seams, embroidered detail, compressed felt forms, practical handmade effects, and subtle stepped cadence.`
- **Style seal:** `Needle-felt stop motion with visible wool fibers, stitched construction, soft compression physics, handmade environmental effects, and subtle stepped cadence.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Smooth plastic skin, liquid CGI surfaces, clean vector edges, invisible fibers.
- **Common failure modes:** Ocean or smoke becoming photoreal; wet glossy wool; felt objects melting instead of compressing.

## Plasticine claymation

- **ID:** `plasticine-claymation`
- **Aliases:** clay animation, plasticine stop motion, sculpted-clay film
- **Medium lock:** Sculpted plasticine characters, props, effects, and miniature sets.
- **Construction:** Forms use hand-shaped clay over armatures, painted clay details, replacement facial pieces, and sculpted practical scenery.
- **Surface behavior:** Fingerprints, thumbed planes, tool grooves, color blending, and small surface dents remain visible.
- **Motion cadence:** Stepped replacement-animation timing with expressive holds and controlled squash-and-stretch.
- **Deformation rules:** Soft clay compresses, stretches, dents, and reforms while anatomy and part count remain stable.
- **Environment inheritance:** Fire, smoke, water, clouds, and debris are sculpted or replaced frame by frame with translucent or colored clay pieces.
- **Imperfection cues:** Fingerprints; thumbed surfaces; tool marks; slight replacement jitter.
- **Compatible tones:** Absurd comedy, warm fantasy, creature performance, playful action.
- **Prompt opening:** `Handcrafted plasticine claymation with sculpted characters and practical miniature sets, visible fingerprints and tool marks, softly thumbed surfaces, colorful practical effects, and replacement-animation timing.`
- **Style seal:** `Plasticine claymation with visible fingerprints, sculpted practical effects, expressive squash-and-stretch, replacement-animation cadence, and handmade miniature charm.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Polished plastic, invisible surface handling, live-action fire or water, hard robotic joints unless required.
- **Common failure modes:** Smooth CGI finish; clay fire becoming photoreal; excessive melting; changing limb count.

## Vintage rubber hose

- **ID:** `vintage-rubber-hose`
- **Aliases:** 1930s cartoon, vintage cel, early theatrical animation
- **Medium lock:** Period hand-inked cel animation with monochrome painted backgrounds and rubber-hose character mechanics.
- **Construction:** Characters use simple ink contours, pie-cut eyes where appropriate, gloved hands, flexible tube limbs, and period production design.
- **Surface behavior:** Ink wobble, cel texture, exposure flicker, dust, scratches, and soft painted background grain.
- **Motion cadence:** Rhythmic elastic cycles, clear anticipation, bouncy follow-through, and theatrical poses.
- **Deformation rules:** Limbs stretch in smooth arcs; bodies squash on impact and rebound; silhouettes stay readable.
- **Environment inheritance:** Smoke, vehicles, architecture, crowds, and impacts use monochrome ink, painted cels, and multiplane staging.
- **Imperfection cues:** Ink-line wobble; nitrate-like flicker; cel dust; registration jitter.
- **Compatible tones:** Whimsical unease, musical comedy, surreal scale, industrial fable.
- **Prompt opening:** `Authentic period rubber-hose cel animation with monochrome ink contours, hand-painted backgrounds, elastic character design, rhythmic movement, subtle exposure flicker, dust, and aged film texture.`
- **Style seal:** `Vintage rubber-hose animation with elastic inked motion, hand-painted cels, rhythmic squash-and-stretch, multiplane staging, and restrained film wear.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Modern glossy 3D, contemporary vector cleanliness, color unless requested, modern typography or logos.
- **Common failure modes:** Generic modern cartoon; excessive damage obscuring action; inconsistent period design; rigid limbs.

## Stylized handcrafted 3D

- **ID:** `stylized-handcrafted-3d`
- **Aliases:** tactile 3D, crafted CGI, stylized animated feature
- **Medium lock:** Authored 3D forms with simplified design, tactile materials, and intentionally handcrafted irregularity.
- **Construction:** Rounded modeled characters, readable rigs, sculpted props, authored fabric and surface shaders, and coherent 3D sets.
- **Surface behavior:** Soft microtexture, subtle wear, modeled seams, controlled subsurface response, and non-uniform crafted detail.
- **Motion cadence:** Smooth feature-animation timing with clear anticipation, expressive facial acting, and deliberate comedic or dramatic holds.
- **Deformation rules:** Controlled rig deformation and squash-and-stretch preserve volume, topology, identity, and costume construction.
- **Environment inheritance:** Effects remain stylized within the same rendering language rather than becoming live action or flat 2D.
- **Imperfection cues:** Slight asymmetry; authored surface wear; modeled seams; restrained stop-motion-inspired settling.
- **Compatible tones:** Mature comedy, cozy drama, expressive character short, cinematic fantasy.
- **Prompt opening:** `Stylized handcrafted 3D animation with rounded authored forms, tactile materials, subtle asymmetry and modeled seams, expressive rigged movement, and warm cinematic rendering.`
- **Style seal:** `Stylized handcrafted 3D with tactile authored materials, expressive rigged motion, controlled squash-and-stretch, subtle irregularity, and cinematic feature-animation timing.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Live-action skin, flat vector shading, textureless plastic, accidental stop-motion jitter. Do not add `no CGI`.
- **Common failure modes:** Generic glossy 3D; photoreal humans; uncontrolled rubber deformation; textureless surfaces.

## Painterly 2D

- **ID:** `painterly-2d`
- **Aliases:** painted 2D action, illustrated feature animation, brush-texture 2D
- **Medium lock:** Layered painterly 2D frames with visible brush texture, strong graphic color, and hand-drawn perspective.
- **Construction:** Characters use stable drawn silhouettes over painted backgrounds with controlled line and brush variation.
- **Surface behavior:** Brush edges, dry texture, pigment variation, selective linework, and composited painted layers.
- **Motion cadence:** Fluid choreography with readable key poses, purposeful smear frames, and energetic but legible transitions.
- **Deformation rules:** Drawn bodies stretch only through designed smear or impact frames and return to stable model sheets.
- **Environment inheritance:** Light blooms, speed trails, smoke, debris, and weather appear as painted strokes or graphic shapes.
- **Imperfection cues:** Brush-edge variation; line-weight change; pigment texture; brief designed smears.
- **Compatible tones:** Action, thriller, fantasy, graphic drama, lyrical montage.
- **Prompt opening:** `Painterly 2D feature animation with layered brush textures, stable drawn silhouettes, bold graphic color, hand-painted backgrounds, expressive line variation, and fluid key-pose choreography.`
- **Style seal:** `Painterly 2D animation with stable illustrated forms, visible brush texture, fluid key-pose choreography, designed smear frames, and cinematic graphic lighting.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Photoreal live action, hard 3D modeling, clean vector uniformity, accidental anatomy drift.
- **Common failure modes:** Style collapsing into anime shorthand; random line jitter; muddy action; smear frames becoming persistent distortion.

## Pearlescent silicone

- **ID:** `pearlescent-silicone`
- **Aliases:** silicone creature, translucent organic animation, soft biomorphic film
- **Medium lock:** Soft translucent silicone-like organisms with pearlescent organic surfaces in a coherent stylized world.
- **Construction:** Characters use molded rounded forms, embedded or coral-like growths, soft appendages, and restrained facial features.
- **Surface behavior:** Subsurface glow, pearlescent color shift, soft translucency, faint mold texture, and gentle surface compression.
- **Motion cadence:** Slow hypnotic motion with hesitant steps, restrained gestures, long holds, and delayed elastic settling.
- **Deformation rules:** Forms compress and rebound softly while volume, identity, and appendage count stay stable.
- **Environment inheritance:** Sand, haze, particles, and architecture share soft organic or pearlescent qualities without turning the scene photoreal.
- **Imperfection cues:** Faint mold texture; tiny surface inclusions; uneven pearlescence; delayed elastic settling.
- **Compatible tones:** Melancholy, wonder, quiet unease, contemplative science fantasy.
- **Prompt opening:** `Delicate pearlescent silicone-creature animation with translucent organic forms, soft subsurface glow, faint mold texture, pastel color shifts, volume-preserving compression, and delayed elastic settling.`
- **Style seal:** `Pearlescent silicone creature animation with soft volume-preserving compression, translucent organic surfaces, delayed elastic settling, restrained acting, and hypnotic pacing.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Wet flesh, gore, hard plastic, rigid robotic motion, photoreal human skin.
- **Common failure modes:** Grotesque body horror; jelly-like volume loss; glossy plastic; overly fast acting.

## Wax crayon redraw

- **ID:** `wax-crayon-redraw`
- **Aliases:** crayon animation, colored-pencil morph, living drawing
- **Medium lock:** Wax crayon and colored pencil drawn on visibly textured paper, redrawn continuously from frame to frame.
- **Construction:** Characters and environments use layered pigment, scribbled contours, exposed paper, and hand-built color fields.
- **Surface behavior:** Wax buildup, broken pigment, paper tooth, pressure variation, and loose colored particles remain visible.
- **Motion cadence:** Energetic line boil and continuous redraw with legible silhouettes and smooth directional flow.
- **Deformation rules:** Forms transform by being erased, extended, overwritten, or redrawn; they do not become clean vector or 3D objects mid-transition.
- **Environment inheritance:** Corridors, forests, water, snow, tunnels, light, and voids emerge from the same crayon marks and paper surface.
- **Imperfection cues:** Line boil; pigment buildup; paper grain; imperfect overlapping outlines.
- **Compatible tones:** Dream journey, childhood memory, kinetic music film, surreal transformation.
- **Prompt opening:** `Wax-crayon and colored-pencil animation on textured paper, with visible pigment buildup and paper tooth, imperfect layered outlines, bold handcrafted color, and lively line boil.`
- **Style seal:** `Wax-crayon redraw animation with visible paper grain, pigment buildup, vibrating line boil, seamlessly redrawn environments, and imperfect handcrafted outlines.`
- **Relevant exclusions:** Clean vector lines, polished 3D surfaces, photoreal inserts, abrupt material changes.
- **Common failure modes:** Background switching instead of redrawing; paper texture disappearing; line boil obscuring identity; morphs becoming liquid CGI.

## Custom recipe template

Use this only when no preset matches:

- **ID:** `custom-<kebab-case-medium>`
- **Aliases:** user language and close synonyms
- **Medium lock:** Name what the visible world is made from.
- **Construction:** Define bodies, props, sets, and practical/digital layering.
- **Surface behavior:** Name two to four observable surface traits.
- **Motion cadence:** Define the temporal character of movement.
- **Deformation rules:** State how parts bend, compress, stretch, rotate, or redraw.
- **Environment inheritance:** Translate effects and locations into the same medium.
- **Imperfection cues:** Choose two to four craft artifacts.
- **Compatible tones:** Name tones that fit the mechanics.
- **Prompt opening:** One concise medium-first sentence.
- **Style seal:** One concise closing style sentence.
- **Relevant exclusions:** Only likely contradictions.
- **Common failure modes:** Predict how the medium might drift or become generic.
