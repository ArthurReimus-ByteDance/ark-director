# Hypothetical Dialogue Repairs

Read this only for a naturalness diagnosis or a requested rewrite. These are
**HYPOTHETICAL invented cases**, not real transcripts, approved canon, or
measured model results.

## Case 1 — the words are locked, the delivery is stiff

Established for this example: two close friends on a private call; the user
locks `{Nakarating ka na?}`. The heard performance resembles an announcement.
There is no evidence of a wrong word or pronunciation error.

**Diagnosis:** delivery does not serve the intimate check-in. Changing the
words or generating a separate track does not follow from this evidence.

**Minimal repair:** keep the line and native-audio mode; change only delivery.

```text
She listens for her friend to pick up, lowers her voice, and asks directly,
waiting for an answer: {Nakarating ka na?}
```

Acceptance: exact words remain; the address feels directed to one friend;
there is room for the friend's answer. Whether the output meets this target
must be judged by listening, not by the prompt's presence of a keyword.

## Case 2 — the user authorized a conversational rewrite

Established for this example: the intended meaning is a simple private
arrival check between close friends. The user explicitly requests a shorter,
conversational alternative to this hypothetical draft:

```text
{Nais ko lamang malaman kung nakarating ka na.}
```

**Proposed alternative, not yet approved:**

```text
{Nakarating ka na?}
```

The alternative preserves the arrival question but changes an indirect stated
wish into a direct question. Offer it only when that shift fits the speaker's
intent. If the character is deliberately distant or formal, the original may
be the right choice. Keep it canonical until the rewrite is accepted, or make
the change only within already explicit rewrite authority.

Acceptance: the user agrees the directness and register fit; the line still
asks about arrival rather than making a promise, accusation, or new claim.
Do not report this as a pronunciation fix without pronunciation evidence.
