# Hypothetical music-video repairs

These are constructed teaching examples. No generated result or measured
improvement is claimed. Use them to isolate a change, then review actual media
only within authorized generation scope.

## Quiet repeated chorus

**Brief:** A supplied recording repeats the refrain more softly; keep its intimacy.

**Initial prompt:** “Each chorus doubles the cuts and lighting intensity.”

**Hypothetical failure:** The treatment overrides the quieter vocal and feels like
an unrelated climax.

**Smallest change:** Keep performer, set and composition. Hold the second refrain
in the same framing, with a smaller gesture and one gradual release of the hand.

**Tradeoff:** Less spectacle; the viewer must attend to the performance. Check
whether the repetition feels intentional and supports the quieter phrase.

## Irregular phrase and false precision

**Brief:** The supplied vocal enters at 17.170s and finishes at 19.730s over rubato
piano; the measured onset is not a downbeat.

**Initial prompt:** “Snap the entry to beat one at 18s; cut two frames early.”

**Hypothetical failure:** Lip motion and the editorial accent precede the wrong
phrase, despite a superficially tidy beat grid.

**Smallest change:** Preserve the raw 17.170–19.730s evidence. If useful, label
17–20s as a simplified prompt window separately. Set final sync to the audible
phrase and verify against the master; do not prescribe an early offset.

**Tradeoff:** Less convenient grid editing; more faithful phrasing. A broad model
prompt window still requires editorial verification for precise delivery.

## Continuous counterpoint

**Brief:** One uninterrupted shot of a still performer while the percussion grows
denser; the intended effect is emotional distance.

**Initial prompt:** “Crash zoom and cut to new locations on every drum hit.”

**Hypothetical failure:** Literal audio matching destroys both the one-take request
and its emotional distance.

**Smallest change:** Hold the shot and quiet body language through the increasing
percussion. Let a single deliberate turn occur after the final hit's decay.

**Tradeoff:** Delayed visual release may feel restrained. Judge whether the held
contrast communicates the intended distance, not whether every accent is matched.
