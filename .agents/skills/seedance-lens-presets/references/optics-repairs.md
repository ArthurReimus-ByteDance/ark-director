# Hypothetical Optics Repairs

Use these **HYPOTHETICAL** invented examples only for ambiguity or conflicting
optical direction. They are not measured renders or approved project changes.
Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md) for evidence classes and model boundaries.

## Unclear depth target

Brief: “85mm, f/1.4, the person and the sign should read clearly.” The person is
foreground and the sign is far behind. Camera position and sign wording are
locked. Missing: whether both must be sharp at once or the sign can become
readable in a separate focus beat.

Do not claim the aperture number guarantees both planes. Ask which reading is
intended while preserving the lens request. Offer the smallest alternatives:

- Keep shallow-focus intent and use a separate, explicitly approved focus change.
- Keep both planes readable and propose a deeper-focus visual target, explaining
  that this changes the requested shallow-focus effect.

Do not move the camera or the sign silently. Exact text fidelity remains an
output-QA requirement, independent of the optical phrase.

## “4K anamorphic” with a locked model

Established: the user requested a 4K output and an anamorphic look; the model is
already selected. Current support for 4K is missing or rejected by the live tool.

Return the requested look phrase and a separate unresolved output requirement.
Do not convert “anamorphic” into a resolution control or switch models on the
user's behalf. The caller can offer a verified supported model/output choice.
Preserve the approved lighting and focus; do not insert a flare merely to make
the style word visibly obvious.

## Anamorphic without a bright source

Brief: deep-focus overcast courtyard, existing lighting locked, anamorphic
character requested. There is no requested flare or defocused highlight.

Keep those locks. Describe the requested framing and restrained lens character
without adding a sun, headlight, flare, or blurred background. Whether an output
reads as the intended lens style is judged visually, not by compulsory inclusion
of every preset cue.
